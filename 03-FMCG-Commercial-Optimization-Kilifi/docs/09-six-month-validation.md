## 2. Route Prioritization & Time Allocation Playbook

Field representatives are transitioned from unmapped roaming to structured, segment-prioritized daily routes designed to maximize revenue yield per labor hour:

1. **Large Tier (Supermarkets & Wholesalers)**: Allocated **35-minute structured pitches**. Reps perform complete inventory checks, audit shelf placement, offer pre-approved 7-Day Credit, and establish standing reorder schedules.
2. **Medium Tier (Mini-Marts & Large Dukas)**: Allocated **20–25 minute visits**. Reps present promotional bundles and 7-Day Credit options upon verifying prior payment compliance.
3. **Small Tier (Kiosks & Small Dukas)**: Allocated compressed **10–15 minute reorder visits**. Pitch time is optimized for rapid transactional velocity on a strict cash-on-delivery basis.

## 3. Credit Governance & Default Threshold Monitoring

Extending credit in FMCG field distribution introduces working capital risk. To protect cash flow while expanding sales volume, credit terms are bound by strict operational guardrails:

* **Eligibility Gate**: Only outlets in Large and Medium tiers with at least 3 prior cash transactions are eligible for 7-Day Credit.
* **Credit Ceiling**: Maximum initial credit limit is capped at **KES 15,000** for Large accounts and **KES 5,000** for Medium accounts.
* **Default Stop-Rule**: If an account incurs an unpaid invoice exceeding 14 days post-delivery, further credit sales are automatically suspended across all sales reps until cleared.
* **Portfolio Risk Threshold**: Total regional non-performing debt must not exceed **$1.5\%$** of gross monthly billings.

## 4. Validation Metrics & Operational KPI Governance

| **Conversion Efficiency** | Overall Conversion Rate (`%`) | 58.41% | **$\ge$ 65.00%** | Re-evaluate channel targets if conversion drops below $55\%$. |
