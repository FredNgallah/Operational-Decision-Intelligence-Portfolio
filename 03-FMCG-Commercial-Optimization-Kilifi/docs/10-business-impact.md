## 2. Organizational Capability Transformation

The project transformed key commercial functions within The Client from intuition-driven habits into disciplined, data-backed operational capabilities:

| Operational Function | Legacy "Chaos" State (Jan 2026) | Optimized Institutional State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Route Planning** | Reps roamed territories based on gut feel and proximity. | Algorithmic, segment-prioritized routes targeting high-yield accounts. |
| **Pitch Structure** | Ad-hoc paper brochure presentations with unmeasured duration. | Structured pitch allocation (35 min for priority; 15 min for reorder). |
| **Credit Decisions** | Rep-discretionary credit extended informally to store owners. | System-enforced credit terms governed by automated risk gates. |
| **Performance Visibility**| Weekly verbal summaries with zero transactional traceability. | Daily automated 20-domain dashboard tracking conversion & revenue yield. |
| **Inventory Control** | Frequent stockouts and untracked warehouse spoilage. | Automated FIFO stock ledger with dynamic reorder point alerts. |

## 3. System Sustenance & Digital Integration

To guarantee that the 20-domain operational data system remains clean and active, software guardrails were built directly into daily field operations:

1. **Mobile Sales App Validation**: Mandatory input fields prevent reps from submitting an order without an associated visit ID, store category, and timestamped pitch duration.
2. **Automated Weekly KPI Engine**: A automated Python script processes raw sales, inventory, and delivery logs every Sunday evening, generating executive summary briefs for Monday morning commercial reviews.
3. **Data Governance Protocol**: Territory managers perform bi-weekly data audits, cross-referencing physical stock levels against digital order ledgers to ensure $100\%$ relational data integrity.
