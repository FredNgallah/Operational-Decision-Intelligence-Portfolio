## 2. Key Executive Conclusions

1. **Operation Standardized:** Operationalized 20 data domains into a structured workbook across 6,591 field visits generating KES 11.65M in audited baseline revenue.
2. **Key Commercial Levers:** Identified customer size tier and channel type as critical stratification context, while isolating credit terms and visit duration as primary controllable levers.
3. **Simulated Optimum:** Model predictions favor 7-day credit terms combined with structured 35-minute visits for medium/large tiers.
4. **Validation Phase:** Commencing a 6-month live market validation across 5 sales reps and 2 vehicles in Kilifi County.
