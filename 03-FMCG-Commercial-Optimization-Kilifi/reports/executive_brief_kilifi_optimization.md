# Executive Decision Brief: Commercial Optimization & Operational Standardization

**Client:** A leading Dairy products business  
**Region:** Kilifi County Expansion  
**Baseline Horizon:** January 1, 2026 – June 30, 2026  
**Audited Baseline Revenue:** KES 11,648,320 (6,591 Visits | 3,846 Orders)

---

## Executive Summary

When The Client expanded into Kilifi County, field operations suffered from unstructured territory coverage, ad-hoc sales pitching, and zero operational visibility. Rather than jumping directly to intuition-led sales tactics, this project first made the commercial operation measurable. 

By implementing an audited 20-domain data collection architecture, screening multi-variable drivers, and executing a 2x2 custom experimental design (DOE) in JMP, we isolated the levers that govern commercial yield.

## Summary of Key Findings

1. **Order Value Driver:** Customer size tier and shop type interact significantly ($R^2 \approx 0.487, p < 0.0001$). Large and Medium outlets drive disproportionate revenue volume.
2. **Controllable Levers:** Sales rep tenure and vehicle choice showed negligible explanatory variance. Focus was narrowed to credit terms ($x_1$) and visit duration ($x_2$).
3. **Simulated Model Optimum:** Model profilers indicate candidate operating conditions of **7-Day Credit Terms** and **35-Minute Visit Durations** yield maximal revenue per visit.
4. **Governance Notice:** Simulated DOE findings represent model-predicted hypotheses, currently undergoing a 6-month field validation program across Kilifi routes.
